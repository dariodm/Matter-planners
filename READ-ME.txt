MATTER PLANNERS WEBSITE
=======================

What is in this folder
  index.html   the whole site (text, styles and the edition list are all in this one file)
  img/         the planner page images the site shows
  og.png       the preview image that LinkedIn, iMessage and Slack show when someone shares the link

To put it online (about ten minutes, no code)
  1. Go to app.netlify.com/drop and sign up (free).
  2. Drag this whole folder onto the page. The site is live within a minute at an address like
     something-random.netlify.app. That link already works and can be shared.
  3. To use your own domain: buy one (Cloudflare Registrar, Namecheap or Squarespace Domains, about
     $10 to $20 a year), then in Netlify go to Domain management > Add a domain and follow the steps.
     Netlify adds the security certificate for you.
  4. Open index.html in any text editor, search for YOUR-DOMAIN (it appears twice) and replace it with
     your domain, for example matterplanner.com. Save and drag the folder onto Netlify again.
     This makes the preview image show when the link is shared.

To update later
  Edit index.html in a text editor and drag the folder onto Netlify again. Everything that changes
  often is at the bottom of the file, in plain English:
    EDITIONS       one block per edition: name, blurb, bullets, Amazon link, price, cover color
    TESTIMONIALS   quotes from people who agreed to be quoted; the section appears when there is one
    PHOTOS         your own photographs; drop the files in img/ and list them here
    CONTACT_EMAIL  your email; the bulk-order question and the footer contact appear when it is set
