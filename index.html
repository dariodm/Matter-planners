Questions

Before you order.

Which edition is mine?

If you bill time, the Outside Counsel edition. If you work inside a company's legal department, the In-House edition. If you run a business or a team and want the structure without the legal tools, the Executive edition.

Does it lie flat?

It is a paperback. It opens comfortably on a desk, but it is not a lay-flat binding.

Which pens work?

Ballpoint, gel and pencil are safe choices on the white paper. If you write with a fountain pen, test it on a back page first.

Is any of this legal, tax or medical advice?

No. The calendar and notes are editorial. Dates can change, and local rules, orders and client requirements control. Verify before relying on a printed date.

Can a firm or team order in bulk?

Yes. Write to for pricing on orders of ten or more.
